# Follow-ups

A quote that gets no answer is a lost sale if nobody chases it. With follow-ups on, when the customer does not answer the agent's last email, the agent writes a **short reminder in the same thread**.

## Mailbox settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Days to wait** | Days without an answer before the reminder is written (1 to 90). With no value, follow-ups are off. |
| **Maximum reminders** | How many times in a row the agent may insist (1 to 10; 1 by default). |
| **Send mode of the reminders** | **Draft** or **Automatic send**. If you do not choose, reminders go out the same way as [replies](/en/email/replies). |

The send mode of the reminders is independent of the one for replies. You can, for example, review every reply yourself and let the reminders go out on their own.

## When it writes a reminder

- The last email of the thread was **sent by the agent** and the days to wait have passed. They are calendar days, counted from when the email was really sent.
- The customer **has not written** since. Automatic replies (out of office, read receipts) do not count: they neither cancel the reminder nor restart the wait.
- No person has [taken the thread over](/en/email/human-takeover).
- There are reminders left. If the customer writes again, the count starts over.

With a wait of 3 days and a maximum of 2, a customer who never answers gets a reminder after 3 days, another after 6, and the conversation closes after 9.

<Note>
A reply that is still in Drafts has not reached the customer, so it gets no reminder. The wait starts when you send it.
</Note>

## When it does not

The agent reads the thread before writing. If its last email did not expect an answer (a thank you, a goodbye, a confirmation), it **sends nothing** and notes the reason in the conversation.

There is also no reminder when one of the mailbox's [daily reply limits](/en/email/reply-rules#rules-you-can-change) has been reached: reminders count as replies.

## Reminders as drafts

The reminder is left in Outlook's Drafts folder, inside the thread. Keep in mind:

- Nothing tells you it is there: check Drafts often.
- There is at most **one pending reminder per thread**.
- If the customer writes before you send it, the agent replaces it with its reply.
- If you do not send it, it is **deleted on its own** when the conversation closes, about 3 days later. If you edited it, it is not deleted.

## Reminders sent automatically

They are sent like any automatic reply: they respect the [delay before sending](/en/email/replies#delay-before-sending) and carry the AI notice. If the customer writes while the reminder waits out the delay, it is not sent.
