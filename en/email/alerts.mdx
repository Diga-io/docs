# Alerts

A mailbox can stop answering without anyone noticing. Diga tells your team, in the app and by email, in these cases:

| Alert | When it arrives | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **An email is waiting for a person** | The agent asked for help with an email and did not answer it. | Answer from the mailbox. |
| **The mailbox was disconnected** | Microsoft withdrew access, usually after a password change or because the permission was revoked. | [Connect the mailbox](/en/email/connect-mailbox) again. |
| **We cannot read the mailbox** | The connection is still active, but we have not been able to read the mail for 30 minutes. | Diga keeps trying. If it does not clear, check the account's status in Microsoft 365. |
| **An email was left unanswered** | The agent could not answer an email and will not try again. | Answer it yourself from the mailbox. |

Each alert links to the mailbox or the conversation it refers to and is sent once per incident.

The alert that an email is waiting for a person goes to the project members who can take over conversations. The others go to the members who can view mailboxes. Each person can turn each alert on or off in their notification preferences.
