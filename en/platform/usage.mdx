# Usage and limits

From **Settings > Usage** you can see how much of each plan limit you've consumed. Every section shows a progress bar with the percentage used and the exact figures.

<Note>
The specific limits for each section depend on your plan. Check the [pricing page](https://www.diga.io/pricing) to see yours.
</Note>

## When your plan resets

At the top of the page you'll see a counter showing how long until your plan resets, for example **Your plan will reset in 1 day 3h 9 min**.

When the cycle resets, your included minutes and the rest of your monthly limits are renewed.

<Note>
Melo usage has its **own weekly counter**, independent of your plan cycle.
</Note>

## Total minutes available

This bar combines **the minutes included in your plan** and **the rollover minutes** from the previous month.

- Unused minutes in a month are carried over to the next (**rollover**).
- Rollover is limited to **2x** the minutes included in your plan.
- Rollover minutes are consumed before generating additional usage.

<Warning>
When you reach **100%** of your minutes, your agents stop receiving calls. To keep receiving them you have two options: enable **usage-based billing** or [upgrade your plan](/en/platform/billing#change-plans).
</Warning>

## Usage-based billing

Usage-based billing lets you **keep making and receiving calls after using up the minutes included** in your plan. Minutes consumed beyond your plan are charged at your plan's price per extra minute.

### Enable usage-based billing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the toggle">
    In the minutes section, turn on **Enable usage-based billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in the modal">
    A window opens explaining that additional usage will be charged based on your current plan's rates. Click **Enable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the confirmation">
    You'll see a notification confirming that usage-based billing is enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disable it, turn the toggle off again. You'll get a notification reminding you that your agents will stop once plan limits are reached.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Enabled">
    - When you use up your included and rollover minutes, calls continue.
    - Extra minutes are billed monthly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Disabled">
    - When you use up your minutes, new calls are blocked.
    - No additional charges are generated.
    - You have to wait for the plan reset or upgrade your subscription.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Extra minutes

Right below the toggle you'll see the **Extra minutes** row, with your plan's price per minute and the additional minutes consumed in the current period.

Extra minutes are the usage beyond the minutes included in your plan, and they're billed monthly. Check each plan's price per minute on the [pricing page](https://www.diga.io/pricing).

## Concurrent calls

This section shows **how many calls tried to run at once**. Your plan's cap appears on the top right (**Plan limit**), and below it you'll find three different measures:

| Measure | Period | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand of concurrent calls right now** | Real time (**Live** tag) | How many calls are in progress at this moment, for example `4/5 concurrent calls in use` |
| **Average peak of demand** | Last 30 days | The average of your demand peaks over the last 30 days |
| **Busiest moment** | Last 30 days | The single highest demand peak recorded in the last 30 days |

Having three measures lets you tell a one-off spike from a recurring problem: if the **average peak** is already close to your limit, it wasn't just an odd day.

### Calls above the limit

As long as you stay under your limit you'll see the **no waiting** state: every call comes through right away.

When demand exceeds your plan's cap, the calls left out are marked as **overlimit** and **are not handled**. For example, `6 calls · 1 overlimit` on a plan capped at 5 means there were 6 calls at once and one didn't get through.

Those calls are recorded in the [call log](/en/deployment/monitoring-logs) with the end reason **Concurrency limit reached** or **Queue timed out**.

<Warning>
The concurrent call limit is set by your plan and can't be raised on its own. If you see overlimit recurring, you need a plan with more concurrent calls.
</Warning>

## Melo usage

[Melo](/en/platform/melo) is the AI assistant inside the dashboard. This bar shows your **weekly quota** consumption, based on the number of requests processed when generating responses, creating agents, or editing flows.

When you use up the quota, **Melo stops working** until it renews at the start of the following week.

<Note>
Melo usage **does not consume call minutes**: they are independent limits.
</Note>

## Published flows

Shows how many [flows](/en/build/flows/introduction) you have published against the maximum allowed by your plan.

When you reach the limit you can't publish more flows. Any changes you've made stay **unpublished** until you upgrade your plan.

## Knowledge bases

Shows the storage used by your [knowledge bases](/en/build/knowledge-bases) against the total available in your plan.

If you reach the limit, you won't be able to add new documents until you free up space or upgrade your subscription.

## Usage notifications

Besides the bars, the platform notifies you when you're approaching a limit:

| Notification | When it appears | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **You've used 80% of your minutes** | When you consume 80% of your included minutes | Review your consumption pace and consider enabling usage-based billing |
| **You've reached your minutes plan limit** | When you hit 100% | Upgrade your plan or enable usage-based billing to keep receiving calls |
| **Approaching concurrent call limit** | When few concurrent call slots are left | Consider a plan with more concurrent calls |
| **You've run out of resources** | When your call minutes are used up | Upgrade your plan to keep making calls |
| **You have unpublished changes** | When you reach your plan's maximum of published flows | Upgrade your plan to be able to publish |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see how many minutes I have left?">
    In **Settings > Usage**, in the **Total minutes available** bar. It adds together your plan's included minutes and the rollover minutes from the previous month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I use up all my minutes?">
    If usage-based billing is disabled, your agents stop receiving calls until the plan resets. If it's enabled, calls continue and extra minutes are billed monthly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Melo consume call minutes?">
    No. Melo has its own weekly quota, independent of call minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I avoid unexpected charges?">
    Keep usage-based billing disabled. That way extra minutes are never generated: calls are blocked once your plan is used up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I raise the concurrent call limit without changing plans?">
    No. The number of concurrent calls is determined by your plan. Check the [pricing page](https://www.diga.io/pricing) to see each plan's limit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
